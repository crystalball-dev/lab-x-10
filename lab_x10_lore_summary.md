# Lab X-10 (Bunker X-10) — Lore Summary & Research Guide

**Lab X-10** is the underground laboratory beneath the Radar in *S.T.A.L.K.E.R.: Shadow of Chernobyl*. It houses the machinery behind the **Brain Scorcher**, the psychic emitter that turns every stalker who wanders north of the Army Warehouses into a mindless servant of the Monolith. Its game level id is `l10u_bunker`.

---

## 1. Core Purpose: The Rainbow Emitter
* **Assembled from other labs.** The main components developed in labs **X-18** and **X-16** were sent to X-10 to build a large-scale psychic antenna, codenamed **Rainbow** ("Радуга"). Stalkers call it the Brain Scorcher.
* **A power plant underground.** The complex is mostly empty space filled with **massive power generators**, needed to feed the emitter continuously. The antenna array stands on the surface directly above.
* **Duty cycles.** Documents found on Ghost describe a second "Psi-Brain" mechanism and note that these machines are not always run at full strength: there are periods when their power is lowered so the apparatus can cool down. A stalker named Crazy (the "Psycho") knows the schedule and the safe places to wait out the emissions.

## 2. Access and Occupants
* **The sidetrack.** The lab sits directly below the surface complex and is reached by a **dead-end railway sidetrack** at the antenna grounds.
* **Monolith.** The faction guards the Radar and the Red Forest approaches. After Strelok shuts the emitter down, Monolith squads flood the lab to ambush him, and on the surface Spetsnaz, Duty and Freedom clash with them on the road to Pripyat.
* **Bloodsuckers.** Up to four roam the otherwise empty halls; rodents and more bloodsuckers return later.
* **The keypad door.** A stairwell leads to a locked room with a numeric keypad. The code **342089** comes from a Monolith soldier's PDA: "A number haunts me, as if it's chasing me from a previous life."

## 3. Development History and Naming
* Early builds (notably build 1935) called the level **"Bunker X-10"**, with its entrance elsewhere in the Red Forest; the final game moved the entrance to the antenna grounds.
* The PDA diary refers to the lab as **Lab X19** in all but one place, a leftover from an older story draft. The *S.T.A.L.K.E.R. 2* Interactive Almanac confirms that X-10 and X-19 are two separate laboratories, X-19 being under the Chernobyl NPP itself.
* Turning off the Brain Scorcher is the main quest "Turn Off The Brain Scorcher", the gateway to Pripyat and the endgame.
